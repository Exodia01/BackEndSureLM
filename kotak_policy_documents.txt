================================================================================
KOTAK MAHINDRA INSURANCE - DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS BY POLICY TYPE
================================================================================

SOURCE: Extracted from policy PDFs in test folder + research on standard 
        underwriting requirements for Kotak Mahindra Life Insurance policies.

================================================================================
1. KOTAK TERM INSURANCE (New Policy)
================================================================================

IDENTITY PROOF:
  - Aadhaar Card
  - Passport  
  - PAN Card
  - Voter ID

ADDRESS PROOF:
  - Utility bills (electricity, telephone)
  - Ration Card
  - Passport

INCOME PROOF:
  - Salary slips (last 3 months)
  - Form 16
  - Income Tax Returns (ITR) for last 2 years
  - Bank statements for last 6 months

AGE PROOF:
  - PAN Card
  - Aadhaar Card
  - Passport
  - Birth Certificate

MEDICAL REPORTS:
  - Blood test reports (for certain age brackets or sum assured amounts)
  - Medical history declarations
  - Physical examination reports

RECENT PHOTOGRAPHS:
  - Passport-sized photographs

APPLICATION FORMS:
  - Completed and signed proposal form
  - Medical report forms (if applicable)

================================================================================
2. KOTAK LIFE INSURANCE (Policy-Based - Endowment, Moneyback, Savings Plans)
================================================================================

STANDARD DOCUMENTS (All Policies):
  - Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card, or Voter ID
  - Address Proof: Utility bills, Ration Card, or Passport
  - Income Proof: Salary slips (last 3 months), Form 16, ITR (2 years), 
                  Bank statements (6 months)
  - Age Proof: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Birth Certificate
  - Recent Photographs: Passport-sized photographs

POLICY-SPECIFIC DOCUMENTS:
  
ENDOWMENT PLANS (e.g., Classic Endowment Plan, Guaranteed Savings Plan):
  - Completed proposal form
  - Medical reports (if age > 45 or sum assured > specified limit)
  - Bank details for premium payment
  - Nomination form

MONEYBACK PLANS (e.g., Premier Moneyback Plan):
  - Same as above + policy-specific questionnaire

SAVINGS PLANS (e.g., Assured Savings Plan, SmartLife Plan):
  - Risk disclosure form (if investment component > 15% of premium)
  - Investment objective declaration
  - Portfolio statement (if existing policies with insurer)

ULIP PLANS (e.g., Wealth Optima Plan, Fortune Maximiser):
  - Investor questionnaire
  - Risk profile assessment
  - Portfolio details (for fund switching options)
  - KYC acknowledgment

PENSION PLANS (e.g., Assured Pension Plan, Premier Pension Plan):
  - Annuity option selection form
  - NomineeDetails for annuity payments
  - Bank account details for annuity payouts
  - Pre-retirement counseling acknowledgment (if applicable)

MICRO INSURANCE PLANS (e.g., Sampoorn Bima Micro Insurance):
  - Simplified application form
  - Income declaration (self-declared)
  - Community verification (for group plans)

4. KOTAK CAR INSURANCE (Own Damage / Comprehensive)
================================================================================

VEHICLE DOCUMENTS:
  - Registration Certificate (RC) copy
  - Previous Insurance Policy document (for renewal)
  - Fitness Certificate
  - Permit (if applicable for commercial vehicles)

IDENTITY & ADDRESS PROOF:
  - Aadhaar Card
  - PAN Card
  - Passport
  - Driving License

RECENT PHOTOGRAPHS:
  - Passport-sized photographs of vehicle owner

OTHER DOCUMENTS:
  - Quote/Proposal form
  - Factory/Aptitude certificate (if modified_vehicle)
  - NOC from financier (if vehicle is hypothecated)

7. KOTAK HOUSEHOLD INSURANCE (New Policy)
================================================================================

PROPERTY DOCUMENTS:
  - Property ownership documents (Sale deed, Agreement)
  - Property tax receipt
  - Previous insurance policy (if renewal)

IDENTITY & ADDRESS PROOF:
  - Aadhaar Card (primary)
  - Passport
  - Voter ID

RECENT PHOTOGRAPHS:
  - Passport-sized photographs

INVENTORY DOCUMENTS (for high-value items):
  - Purchase invoices for valuables
  - Assessment reports for jewelry/art work
  - Photographic evidence of property/items

APPLICATION FORMS:
  - Proposal form
  - Inventory schedule
  - Declaration forms

  [✓] Address proof
  [✓] Identity proof

  [✓] Recent passport-sized photographs

  [✓] Bank account details for premium payments

  □ Pre-policy health check-up (Health Insurance)

10. DOCUMENT SUBMISSION METHODS
================================================================================

ONLINE SUBMISSION:
  - Upload scanned copies of documents via insurer portal
  - E-sign proposal form using Aadhaar OTP
  - Digital medical reports from empanelled labs
  
OFFLINE SUBMISSION:
  - Submit physical documents at branch/office
  - Medical tests at empanelled diagnostic centers
  - Agent-assisted submission

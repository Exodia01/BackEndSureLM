## 🚀 Getting Started in 3 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Sign Up
Tell us about yourself:
- Your name and contact details
- Where you work (village/town)
- What communities you serve

No prior insurance knowledge needed!

### Step 2: Enter a Household's Details
For each family, provide:
- Households income level
- Number of family members
- Any specific needs (elderly care, children's education, health concerns)

We'll automatically suggest the best policies.

### Step 3: Share With The Family
Show them the recommendations in their language.
Guide them through document collection and submission—we handle the rest.

## 💡 Key Features

### 1. AI Policy Recommendations 🧠
Upload household information, and our AI analyzes what policies make sense.

**Example uses:**
- "Which life insurance works for a farmer with ₹8,000/month income?"
- "Health plans that cover pre-existing conditions after waiting period"
- "Policies with maturity benefits for children's education"

### 2. Document Scanning (KYC) 📸
Scan and extract information from:
- Aadhaar cards
- PAN cards
- Voter ID
- Income certificates
- Ration cards

### 3. CRM with Smart Reminders 📅
Automatic tracking of important dates:
- Family members' birthdays (send wishes!)
- Upcoming premium payments (urgent alerts)
- Policy expiration dates

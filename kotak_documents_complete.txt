================================================================================
IRDAI MANDATORY KYC DOCUMENTS (APPLIES TO ALL POLICY TYPES)
================================================================================

1. IDENTIFICATION PROOF (ANY ONE):
   - Aadhaar Card (Original + Self-attested copy) [PREFERRED]
   - Passport (Self-attested copy)
   - Voter ID Card (Self-attested copy)
   - Driving License (Self-attested copy)
   - PAN Card (Mandatory for policies with annual premium > ₹50,000 or sum assured > ₹50 Lakhs)

2. ADDRESS PROOF (ANY ONE):
   - Aadhaar Card (Self-attested copy) [MOST ACCEPTED]
   - Passport (Self-attested copy)
   - Voter ID Card (Self-attested copy)
   - Driving License (Self-attested copy)
   - utility bills (electricity, water, telephone, gas - not older than 3 months)
   - Bank statement/passbook with address (not older than 3 months)
   - Ration card (Self-attested copy)

3. AGE PROOF (REQUIRED FOR CHILDREN/DEPENDENTS):
   - Birth Certificate
   - Maternity/Child Health Card
   - Aadhaar Card (if age is clearly mentioned)
   - Marksheet of Class X or equivalent

4. INCOME PROOF (APPLIES TO POLICIES REQUIRING INCOME VERIFICATION):
   - Salary Slips (last 3 months) for salaried individuals
   - Form 16/ITR copies (latest applicable year)
   - Business Income Certificate (for self-employed, audited financials may be required)
   - Bank statements (last 6 months)
   - Employment appointment letter with salary details

5. PHOTOGRAPHS:
   - Recent passport-size color photographs (2-4 copies depending on policy type)
   - Background: White or light-colored
   - Size: 3.5cm x 3.5cm minimum

6. SIGNED APPLICATION FORM:
   - Policy application form (filled and signed by proposer, insured, and nominee)
   - Nomination form
   - KYC acknowledgment
   - Medical questionnaire (if applicable)

================================================================================
1. TERM INSURANCE POLICY DOCUMENTS
================================================================================

IDENTITY PROOF:
- PAN Card (compulsory) + Aadhaar Card (preferred)
- Passport/Driving License/Voter ID as backup

ADDRESS PROOF:
- Original Aadhaar card or recent utility bills
- Passbook with bank account details

AGE PROOF:
- Birth certificate or class X marksheet
- For senior citizens: Pension book, passport, or birth certificate

INCOME PROOF:
- Not always required for term plans (pure protection plan)
- May be requested for high sum assured (> ₹50 Lakhs) to assess affordability
- Salary slips/ITR copies if requested

MEDICAL REQUIREMENTS:

BY AGE GROUP:
- BELOW 18 YEARS: Medical test rarely required unless pre-existing conditions suspected
- 18-35 YEARS: Usually no medical test for standard term plans with sum assured ≤ ₹50 Lakhs
- 36-45 YEARS: Minor medical tests may be required for sum assured > ₹25 Lakhs
- 46-55 YEARS: Comprehensive medical tests often mandatory regardless of sum assured
- 56+ YEARS: Mandatory comprehensive medical tests

COMMON MEDICAL TESTS:
- Blood tests (HB, FBS, PPBS, LFT, RFT, Lipid Profile)
- Urine tests (Routine and microalbuminuria)
- ECG (especially for age > 40 or if family history of heart disease)
- Chest X-ray (for age > 50 or if smoker/alcoholic)
- TMT (Treadmill Test) for age > 45 with risk factors
- Ultrasonography (if indicated)

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS LIST:
1. Completed and signed proposal form
2. KYC documents (PAN, Aadhaar)
3. Medical report form (filled by doctor if medical test required)
4. Photocopy of previous insurance policy (if any, for disclosure)
5. Proof of income (for high sum assured)
6. Two passport-sized photographs
7. Nomination form
8. Doctor's prescription and medical records (if pre-existing conditions declared)

SPECIAL NOTES:
- Term plans often have easier documentation than savings plans
- Non-medical term plans available for smaller sums (typically ≤ ₹25 Lakhs for age < 35)
- Digitalterm plans may accept e-KYC with Aadhaar OTP verification

================================================================================
2. ENDOWMENT PLANS DOCUMENTS
================================================================================

IDENTITY PROOF:
- PAN Card (mandatory) + Aadhaar Card (required)
- Backup: Passport/Driving License/Voter ID

ADDRESS PROOF:
- Original Aadhaar card or bank statement/passbook
- Utility bills accepted for recent residential address

AGE PROOF:
- Birth certificate for insured and nominees (especially minors)
- Class X marksheet as secondary proof
- For senior citizens: Pension book, passport, or aadhaar

INCOME PROOF:
- Generally required due to premium payment capacity assessment
- Salary slips (last 3 months) or Form 16/ITR for salaried
- Business documents for self-employed
- Bank statements (last 6 months)

MEDICAL REQUIREMENTS:

BY AGE GROUP AND SUM ASSURED:
- BELOW 18 YEARS: Minimal or no medical tests
- 18-35 YEARS: May require basic medical examination if sum assured > ₹25 Lakhs
- 36-45 YEARS: Often required for sum assured > ₹20 Lakhs
- 46+ YEARS: Medical tests almost always required

MEDICAL TESTS COMMONLY REQUIRED:
- Blood tests (HbsAg, FBS, LFT, RFT)
- Urine routine examination
- ECG (mandatory for age > 35, or withany reported symptoms)
- Chest X-ray (if smoker or > 40 years)
- Basic physical examination by registered medical practitioner

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS LIST:
1. Completed proposal form
2. Proof of identity and address (PAN + Aadhaar mandatory)
3. Age proof for all proposers/insured
4. Medical test report (if required based on age/sum assured)
5. Incomeproof (for premium affordability assessment)
6. Recent passport-sized photographs (typically 2-4 copies)
7. Nomination form
8. Bank details for premium payment (cancelled cheque or account details)

SPECIAL FEATURES:
- Endowment plans require more rigorous documentation than term plans
- Medical underwriting is stricter due to savings component
- Pre-policy medical check-ups may be scheduled after application submission

3. MONEYBACK PLANS DOCUMENTS
================================================================================

IDENTITY PROOF:
- PAN Card (compulsory)
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory for e-KYC verification)

ADDRESS PROOF:
- Original Aadhaar card or recent bank statement/passbook
- Utility bills not older than 3 months accepted as secondary proof

AGE PROOF:
- Birth certificate is primary requirement
- Class X marksheet as alternative
- For minors: Maternity/child health record

INCOME PROOF:
- Required due to higher premium payments
- Salary slips (last 3 months) for salaried individuals
- ITR copies or business income statement for self-employed
- Bank statements showing regular transactions

MEDICAL REQUIREMENTS:

BY AGE GROUP:
- BELOW 18 YEARS: Basic medical examination recommended but not always mandatory
- 18-35 YEARS: Medical tests often required if sum assured > ₹20 Lakhs
- 36-45 YEARS: Comprehensive medical examination typically required
- 46+ YEARS: Full medical check-up almost always required

SPECIFIC MEDICAL TESTS FOR MONEYBACK PLANS:
- Blood glucose (FBS, PPBS)
- Cholesterol and lipid profile
- Liver function tests (LFT)
- Kidney function tests (RFT)
- ECG (mandatory for adults)
- Chest X-ray (routine for age > 40)
- Hemoglobin test
- Urine routine and microscopic examination

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS LIST:
1. Filled proposal form with full declarations
2. KYC documents (PAN, Aadhaar - scanned copies for online, originals for offline)
3. Medical examination report (Form required by insurer)
4. Income proof documents
5. Proof of age for proposer and nominees
6. Bank account details (cancelled cheque leaf or account statement)
7. Passport-sized photographs (minimum 2, often 4 required)
8. Nomination form signed by proposer
9. Pre-existing disease disclosure form (if applicable)

SPECIAL NOTES:
- Moneyback plans have survival benefits, requiring stricter underwriting
- Documentation process may be more detailed than standard endowment plans
- Medical assessment is typically conducted at approved diagnostic centers

4. ULIP (UNIT LINKED INSURANCE PLANS) DOCUMENTS
================================================================================

IDENTITY PROOF:
- PAN Card (Mandatory - required by SEBI for investment components)
- Aadhaar Card (compulsory for e-KYC and mutual fund investments)

ADDRESS PROOF:
- Original Aadhaar card or recent bank statement/passbook
- Utility bills as secondary proof

AGE PROOF:
- Birth certificate
- Class X marksheet or equivalent certificate

INCOME PROOF:
- Salary slips (last 3 months) - for salaried
- ITR copies and financial statements - for self-employed
- Bank statements (last 6 months) showing investment history
- Details of existing investments may be requested

MEDICAL REQUIREMENTS:

BY AGE GROUP AND FUND CHOICE:
- BELOW 18 YEARS: Minimal or no medical examination
- 18-35 YEARS: Medical test usually not required for equity-oriented funds
- 36-45 YEARS: May require basic medical tests if choosing balanced/hybrid funds
- 46+ YEARS: Comprehensive medical check-up recommended, mandatory for high sum assured

COMMON MEDICAL TESTS:
- Blood tests (HB, FBS, Lipid Profile)
- Urine routine examination
- ECG (mandatory for age > 35, especially with investment component)
- Basic physical examination including weight, height, BP measurement

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS LIST:
1. Completed ULIP proposal form (special format from insurer)
2. PAN Cardcopy (for insurance + investment components)
3. Aadhaar copy and e-KYC acknowledgment
4. Age proof documents
5. Medical examination report (if required)
6. Incomeproof for premium payment capacity
7. Bank details for premium payments and maturity proceeds (cancelled cheque leaf)
8. Photographs (typically 2 copies, some insurers require more)
9. Nomination form
10. Risk profile assessment form (SEBI-mandated for investment component)

SPECIAL ULIP REQUIREMENTS:
- Separate KYCacknowledgment for the insurance and investment portions
- Risk disclosure documents to be signed by proposer
- Fund selection forms indicating investment preference
- For online ULIPs: Video KYC may be accepted

5. PENSION/ANNUITY PLANS DOCUMENTS
================================================================================

IDENTITY PROOF:
- PAN Card (compulsory)
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory)

ADDRESS PROOF:
- Original Aadhaar card or recent utility bills/bank statements
- Government-issued address proof

AGE PROOF:
- Birth certificate is primary requirement
- Class X marksheet as secondaryproof
- For senior citizens: Pension book, passport, or aadhaar

INCOME PROOF:
- Mandatory for all pension plans to assess annuity requirements
- Salary slips (last 3 months) - salaried individuals
- ITR copies and audited financials - self-employed
- Bank statements (last 12 months) to show consistent income stream
- Details of existing retirement benefits/pensions

MEDICAL REQUIREMENTS:

BY AGE GROUP:
- BELOW 18 YEARS: Rarely required for pension plans
- 18-35 YEARS: May require basic medical examination if deferred annuity plan
- 36-45 YEARS: Medical check-up often required, especially for immediate annuities
- 46+ YEARS: Comprehensive medical examination almost always mandatory

COMMON MEDICAL TESTS:
- Blood tests (HBA1C, Lipid Profile, LFT, RFT)
- ECG with interpretation report
- Chest X-ray (routine for age > 45)
- Urine routine and protein estimation
- Eye check-up (especially for elderly)
- Dental examination (for some plans)

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS LIST:
1. Completed pension plan proposal form
2. KYC documents (PAN + Aadhaar)
3. Age proof for proposer
4. Medical examination report (if required based on age and plan type)
5. Incomeproof documents
6. Bank account details for premium payment and annuity receipts (cancelled cheque)
7. Photographs (typically 2-4 copies)
8. Nomination form and emergency contact details
9. Details of existing retirement benefits (optional but recommended)

SPECIAL PENSION PLAN REQUIREMENTS:
- Annuity calculation forms may be required
- Medical assessment often includes life expectancy evaluation
- For NPS-linked pension plans: Additional NPS KYC documents
- Senior citizen plans may require additional geriatric assessments

6. HEALTH INSURANCE DOCUMENTS (KOTAK MAHINDRA GENERAL INSURANCE)
================================================================================

IDENTITY PROOF:
- PAN Card (compulsory for all proposers)
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory and preferred)

ADDRESS PROOF:
- Original Aadhaar card or recent utility bills/bank statements
- Ration card accepted in some cases

AGE PROOF:
- Birth certificate or class X marksheet
- Fordependents: Birth certificates with parent names
- For senior citizens: Passport or aadhaar

INCOME PROOF:
- Not always required for health insurance (unlike life plans)
- May be requested for high sum assured policies (> ₹10 Lakhs)
- Salary slips or ITR copies if income verification required

MEDICAL REQUIREMENTS:

BY AGE GROUP:
- BELOW 6 MONTHS: Medical examination mandatory for infants
- 6 MONTHS - 17 YEARS: Basic health check-up may be required, especially with pre-existing conditions
- 18-35 YEARS: Often no medical test for standard plans < ₹5 Lakhs sum assured
- 36-45 YEARS: Optionalmedical test may be requested based on lifestyle/health questionnaire
- 46+ YEARS: Mandatory medical examination
- 51+ YEARS: Comprehensive health check-up almost always required

COMMON MEDICAL TESTS BY INSURER:
- Blood tests (HB, FBS, LFT, RFT, Lipid Profile)
- Urine routine and microalbuminuria
- ECG (mandatory for age > 35 or withanyreported symptoms)
- Chest X-ray (routine for age > 40)
- TMT (Treadmill Test) for age > 45 or with cardiac risk factors
- Ultrasound abdomen (forage > 40 or if symptomatic)
- Pap smear and mammography (for female proposers above specified ages)

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS LIST:
1. Completed health insurance proposal form
2. KYC documents (PAN + Aadhaar for all proposers and adults covered)
3. Medical examination reports (if required)
4. Age proof for insured persons
5. Photographs of all proposed insured individuals (passport size, white background)
6. Pre-existing disease disclosure form (critical - must be truthfully filled)
7. Hospitalization records ifany preexisting conditions disclosed
8. Bank details for premium payment and cashless network hospital verification

SPECIAL HEALTH INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS:
- Health questionnaires are extremely detailed and critical for claim approval
- Pre-policy check-ups may be scheduled at diagnostic centers empanelled with insurer
- For group health insurance: Employer NOC and employee details required
- For NRIs: Additional documents including overseas medical reports may be accepted

7. MICRO INSURANCE PLANS DOCUMENTS
================================================================================

IDENTITY PROOF:
- Aadhaar Card (primary and preferred)
- BPL card with photo (accepted for low-income groups)
- Voter ID/Driving License as alternatives

ADDRESS PROOF:
- Aadhaar card or ration card
- Self-declaration of address accepted in many cases
- Gram Panchayat certification may be accepted

AGE PROOF:

INCOME PROOF:

MEDICAL REQUIREMENTS:

BY AGE GROUP:
- BELOW 18 YEARS: Minimal or no medical examination
- 18-35 YEARS: Often no medical tests required
- 36+ YEARS: May require basic physical examination but not extensive testing

DOCUMENTATION SIMPLIFICATION FOR MICRO INSURANCE:
1. Simplified proposal form (often in vernacular language)
2. Aadhaar-based self-declaration
3. Biometric verification at enrollment centers
4. Minimal paperwork designed for low-literacy populations
5. Self-attested documents rather than notarized ones

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS LIST:
1. Simplified application form (may be fillable at kiosk/center)
2. Aadhaar card copy or self-declaration with witness signature
3. Age proof (self-certified in many cases)
4. Photographs of proposer and family members (often 1-2 copies suffice)
5. Nomination form (simplified format)

SPECIAL MICRO INSURANCE FEATURES:
- Digital onboarding via mobile apps with Aadhaar-based e-KYC
- Paperless documentation where feasible
- Agent-assisted document collection in rural areas
- Group policies for SHGs (Self-Help Groups) may have different requirements

GENERAL DOCUMENTATION BEST PRACTICES
================================================================================

1. DIGITAL vs OFFLINE SUBMISSION:
   - Digital: Scanned copies (PDF/JPG), e-KYC with Aadhaar OTP, video KYC accepted
   - Offline: Original + attested copies required for verification

2. SIGNATURE REQUIREMENTS:
   - proposer must sign all forms in same handwriting as KYC documents
   - Witnesses may be required for certain declarations
   - Minors cannot sign; guardian signs on their behalf

3. NOTARIZATION REQUIREMENTS:
   - Not always mandatory but recommended for address proofs in some cases
   - Attested copies preferred over self-attested for older documents
   - For NRI customers: Embassy-attested documents may be required

4. NRI DOCUMENTATION ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:
   - Valid passport copy with visa page
   - Proof of overseas address (utility bill, bank statement)
   - PIO/OCI card if applicable
   - Indian address proof for contact purposes
   - NRE/NRO account details for premium payments and maturity proceeds

5. MINOR NOMINEE DOCUMENTATION:
   - Birth certificate of minor nominee
   - Guardian's KYC documents
   - Guardianshipcertificate (if applicable)
   - photographs of both minors and guardians

6. DOCUMENT VALIDITY:
   - Address proofs: Must be within 3 months validity
   - Medical reports: Typically valid for 30-90 days from date of test
   - PAN/Aadhaar: No expiry but must be valid at time of proposal

## 🎯 Executive Summary

**The Opportunity:**
- 61% of rural India lacks health insurance coverage
- Traditional insurance channels fail to reach grassroots communities
- Massive unmet demand in Tier 2/3 towns and villages

**Our Solution:**
SureLM empowers local agents with AI to bring the right insurance policies to families across India. We bridge the gap between insurers/banks and rural communities through:

✅ **AI-Powered Matching** - Context-aware recommendations  
✅ **Multilingual Support** - Work in any regional language  
✅ **Offline-First Platform** - Works without constant internet  
✅ **Document Processing** - OCR for Aadhaar, PAN, income certs  

**Market Size:** $3.5B+ annual opportunity in rural insurance

## 🔄 Product Architecture Overview

### Core Components:

#### 1. AI Recommendation Engine
- Hybrid retrieval: SQL FTS + Vector Search + User History
- Processes queries in 3-5 seconds
- Supports multilingual responses (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi)

#### 2. Document Processing Pipeline
- Multi-modal OCR via vision models
- Entity extraction for key fields
- Confidence scoring and human review flagging

#### 3. Agent CRM Platform
- Lead management with status tracking
- Automated reminders (birthdays, premiums, follow-ups)
- Performance analytics dashboard
